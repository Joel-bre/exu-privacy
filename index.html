Exu — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

What Exu does

Exu is a floating voice keyboard that transcribes your speech into text and inserts it into any app on your device. All processing happens entirely on your device — nothing leaves it.

Data we collect

None. Exu does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, audio recordings, or transcribed text.

Microphone

Exu uses your microphone only while you are actively recording (tap to start, tap to stop). Audio is processed locally by an on-device AI model and immediately discarded. It is never saved or sent anywhere.

Accessibility Service

Exu uses Android's Accessibility Service solely to insert transcribed text into the focused input field of whichever app you are using — the same action a keyboard performs. Exu does not read screen content beyond identifying the active text field.

Internet access

Exu uses the internet only to download AI model files during initial setup. No user data is transmitted.
